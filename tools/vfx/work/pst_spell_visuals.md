# PST spell visuals survey

Generated by `tools/vfx/work/survey.py` (rerun it to refresh). Data: classic PST at `C:\Program Files (x86)\Black Isle\Torment` (chitin.key + override, Fixpack + UB) layered the way GemRB searches it: `gemrb/override/pst` > `gemrb/override/shared` > game `override` > chitin.key > `gemrb/unhardcoded/pst` > `gemrb/unhardcoded/shared`.

Important context: **classic PST ships no PRO and no VVC files.** Torment.exe hardcoded its projectiles. Every PRO and VVC below is a GemRB reconstruction in `gemrb/unhardcoded/pst` (plus generic ones in `unhardcoded/shared`), together with 43 helper SPLs that those projectiles fire as their payload (`successSpell` at PRO offset 0x4C). `PROJECTL.IDS` is **not present** in the game data; GemRB maps projectile numbers with `gemrb/unhardcoded/pst/gemprjtl.ids`. A SPL extended header stores projectile N, `SPLImporter` decrements it, so the PRO is `gemprjtl[N-1]`.

## Key findings

- Effective counts: 177 SPL, 213 PRO, 20 VVC, 2591 BAM, 728 ITM. Every PRO and VVC is GemRB's; the original game hardcoded them in Torment.exe.
- 16 purely visual opcodes account for 660 of 2243 feature blocks in SPL+ITM. PlayBAM1 (0xBB) alone is 282, the most used opcode in the game.
- PST play-BAM opcodes are 0xBB-0xBF (187-191), not 186-190: 0xBA is SetStatus. 0xBB is `fx_play_bam_blended`; 0xBC-0xBF all run `fx_play_bam_not_blended` with flag bits in p2. They appear 442 times; 0xBE (PlayBAM4) is never used.
- 10 spell BAMs have frames 590+ px wide (PST runs at 640x480): S091PHNX (640x480, 132 frames), S075WDSH (640x480, 32 frames), S052CONE (640x480, 200 frames), S087BLST (640x408, 96 frames), S091ARRS (640x408, 32 frames), S091GATE (640x408, 1 frame), S092CONF (607x480, 64 frames), S914HGBL (591x406, 5 frames), S914RUNE (603x398, 50 frames), S083BGCL (640x231, 64 frames).
- The multi-stage cinematics (Celestial Host, Rune of Torment) are GemRB 2DA sequences (`chost.2da`, `rune.2da`) played by opcode 0xC5 MultipleVVC (`fx_multiple_vvc` in PSTOpcodes.cpp) from GemRB helper SPLs.
- Projectile light spots: GemRB draws one only with travel flag 0x40; 30 PROs qualify, 28 more carry unused light values.
- Several visuals never play in GemRB as shipped (section 5c): Chain Lightning Storm's `lstorm.2da` and Blade Storm's BSTORM2 (fragments without PAF_SECONDARY), Deathbolt's MultipleVVC `DBOLT` (no such resource), Abyssal Fury and Blacksphere projectiles (no PRO), Meteor's `<stoned>` fragments. Found by reading data and `Projectile.cpp`; not checked in game.
